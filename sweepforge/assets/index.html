What the geometry means

Each path point is one equal step of motor phase. Drawing resamples a closed path at equal arc length, which assigns a timing. The positive-branch crank-rocker has a fixed 80 mm ground span. Orange BC, BP and CP bars form one rigid tool frame. Colored sweeps are sampled for display; the clearance result uses interval enclosures across the whole revolution.

Scope of the calculation

Rigid planar kinematics, a positive assembly branch, circular obstacles and capsule-shaped bars. Dimensional tolerance covers ground and r/p/q/u/v lengths plus bar/tool radii. Joint contacts and self-collision between links are excluded. No loads, friction, flex, assembly play or physical safety validation.

Clear means every checked shape is separated under this declared model. Collision has a nominal geometric witness. Unresolved includes tangency, uncertain geometry and exhausted subdivision budget. Exported flat patterns are conceptual and need process and assembly review.

Path synthesis has strong precedents in MotionGen, Pyslvs and academic mechanism design. See the repository method document for prior work and the exact algorithm.
